Where the need is greatest, right now

Sudan and Eastern DRC are facing the world's worst humanitarian crises.

Over 13.6 million people displaced in Sudan. More than 7 million in Eastern DRC. Millions more caught in the instability spreading across the Sahel and the Horn of Africa. Tumaini Africa exists to grow toward this need, honestly and one funded step at a time.

The main picture

Three crises, one widening emergency

This is what is actually happening right now — sourced from the UN and the humanitarian organisations working inside these crises.

SUDAN

13.6M+ displaced

Three years into civil war between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the RSF, over 30 million people need aid and more than 20 areas are in or near famine.

EASTERN DRC

7M+ displaced

Decades of conflict, reignited by the M23 rebel advance in 2025. Over 100 armed groups remain active, compounded by an ongoing Ebola outbreak.

SAHEL & HORN

24M+ in need

Violence spreading from Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger into coastal West Africa, while analysts warn of a genuine risk of renewed Ethiopia–Eritrea war.

100%local, ground-level delivery

Tumaini Africa is a community-founded initiative built around a simple belief: the people closest to a problem are best placed to help solve it, if they have the resources to act. Since 2014, we have grown one neighbourhood, one relationship, and one family at a time — always with the same commitment to transparency and accountability as we work toward becoming a fully registered, independently audited charity.

We exist to serve four groups whose needs are urgent but often overlooked in larger, more distant aid programmes: children living and working on the street, widows rebuilding a household alone, people who are sick and cannot reach or afford care, and children with no classroom to learn in at all.

This is a call to the whole world. Wherever generosity lives — across every city, continent, and community — we invite you to stand with us and help carry this work forward.

Our work

Four pillars, one community

Each pillar is a distinct programme with its own budget and outcomes, so every gift can be directed to what matters most to you.

01 / SHELTER

Street children

Safe overnight shelter, daily meals, trauma-informed care, and a supported path back into a classroom rather than back onto the street.

02 / DIGNITY

Widows

Skills training, small start-up grants for income-generating work, and a support circle so no widow has to rebuild a household completely alone.

03 / CARE

The sick

Transport to the nearest clinic, essential medicine, and follow-up visits for families who would otherwise go without treatment entirely.

04 / EDUCATION

Education for the less fortunate

For too many children, there is no classroom — only open ground beneath a tree. We provide two daily meals, reading and writing materials, and cover school fees, so that poverty never has to be the reason a child stops learning.
